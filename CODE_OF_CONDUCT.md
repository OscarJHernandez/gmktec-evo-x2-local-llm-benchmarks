# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include
demonstrating empathy, being respectful of differing opinions, giving and
gracefully accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is best for the
community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, personal attacks, publishing
private information, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered
inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainers through the repository
contact channels. Reports will be handled confidentially and reviewed fairly.
Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this code.

This code of conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
